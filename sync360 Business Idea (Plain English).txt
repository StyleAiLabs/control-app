Here’s the core business idea about this project in plain terms:

We are building a vertical AI operations platform for service businesses, starting with trades and similar admin-heavy businesses.

The product works like this:

A business signs up, gets its own Digital Employee / AI Office Manager, and that AI handles repetitive back-office work such as:
	•	email triage
	•	booking coordination
	•	quote drafting
	•	invoice/admin follow-up
	•	customer communication workflows

Under the hood, each customer gets a provisioned tenant environment with preconfigured skills and workflows, so they do not need to build anything themselves.

So the business model is:

sign up → business onboarding → auto-provision tenant → enable workflows and skill modules -> start using AI staff workflows immediately 

The key strategic idea we refined was this:

It should not be sold as a generic “AI skills marketplace.”

It should be sold as:

industry-specific, ready-to-use AI skills & workflow packs for service businesses.

So instead of saying:
	•	“Pick your AI skills”

you say:
	•	“Get an AI Office Manager for tradies”
	•	“Automate quote follow-up”
	•	“Handle inbox and bookings automatically”

Technically, the platform has two layers:

1. Control App
This is your SaaS layer that manages:
	•	signup
	•	tenant creation
	•	provisioning
	•	skill & workflow module management 
	•	admin oversight
	•	status/health
	•	future billing and lifecycle management

2. Tenant Runtime
Each client gets their own isolated runtime, where OpenClaw powers (we never expose OpenClaw to the customer) the actual AI assistant/workflows.

That means your long-term model is:
	•	one shared control plane
	•	one isolated AI runtime per customer
	•	reusable skill modules and workflow packs by industry
	•	eventually self-serve onboarding

In one sentence:

We are creating a SaaS platform that gives service businesses their own ready-to-use AI employee, starting with high-value admin workflows and delivered through industry-specific tenant environments.

A tighter pitch version would be:

Sync360 gives service businesses a preconfigured AI Office Manager that handles quotes, inboxes, bookings, and admin work through a self-serve multi-tenant platform.
